Unused dataset schemas

Preview of new Vega-Lite specs built from local files that are not currently wired into index.html. Serve the repo root (for example python -m http.server) and open this page. HomeC.csv (~500k rows) and ai_company_adoption.csv (~150k rows) were left out because they are too heavy for a browser preview.

1. Country AI readiness bubble chart

Source: data/aiAdoption/country_ai_index.csv. Digital maturity vs internet penetration; bubble size is 2024 AI patent filings.

2. AI jobs created vs displaced by industry

Source: data/aiAdoption/ai_industry_summary.csv. Grouped bars of average jobs created and displaced.

3. Willingness to adopt smart home tech (UPGRADE-E)

Source: data/upgradeE/UPGRADE-E_V2.csv. Stacked counts of Yes / Maybe / No by US region.

4. Spanish smart-home listing prices (SMASH)

Source: data/doi-10.21950-jtiqq5/smash_2024_v02.tsv. Mean EUR price by English macro-category, coloured by listings count.

5. Casey IoT device register

Source: data/IOT DEVICE REGISTER KAGGLE/iot-device-register.csv. Device counts by suburb and type.

6. Household appliance energy by season

Source: data/smartHomeEnergyConsumption/smart_home_energy_consumption_large.csv. Mean kWh by appliance, coloured by season. This file is large, so the first draw can take a few seconds.

7. Appliance duty cycle

Source: data/smart_home_dataset/preprocessed_dataset.csv. Share of intervals where each appliance is on.

8. Privacy scenario heatmap (raw GDPR answers)

Source: data/GDPR_priv/answers.csv. Number of survey scenarios at each data-class × purpose-class pair. Distinct from the aggregated-person charts already on the dashboard.
